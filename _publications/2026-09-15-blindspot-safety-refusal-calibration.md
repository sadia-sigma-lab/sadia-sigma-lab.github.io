BLINDSPOT is a benchmark for evaluating safety and refusal calibration in long-horizon, tool-using LLM agents. It assesses agent behavior across safe completion, unsafe completion, correct refusal, over-refusal, and indeterminate outcomes under diverse multi-turn attack scenarios.
